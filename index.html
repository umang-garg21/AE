01Mission

Agents propose.Physics decides.

Language models are brilliant at ideas and unreliable at being right. we use them for what they are good at, inside a machine where the rules are code, the judge is simulation, and every decision is on the record.

02The problem

Hardware still moves at the speed of hand-run simulation.

A new cold plate, heat exchanger or thrust chamber takes a specialist team months of CAD, meshing, CFD and redesign. That loop sets the pace for everything that runs hot, from AI data centres to rockets.

Why now
Demand01

Chips crossed a thermal wall

AI accelerators now draw around a kilowatt each. Every new generation needs new liquid-cooling hardware, and needs it fast.

Manufacturing02

Any geometry can be built

Metal additive manufacturing prints lattices and channels no machinist could make. The design space now exceeds what people can search.

AI03

Physics can grade AI

Simulation answers "does this part work?" with a number. That makes engineering one of the few fields where models can learn from reward, not imitation.

03The engines

Three engines. One loop.

Yukti decides what to change. Vega predicts what the change will do. Pramana proves whether the design holds up. Every hand-off is checked by rules and written to a ledger.

01Design Reasoning

Yukti (युक्ति)

reasoning · strategy

Reads the chip brief, the pump and pressure limits and the shop’s rules. Proposes a CAD revision, says what it expects to improve, and decides what to change next when a check fails.

05Inside a design
01 / Design · Yukti (युक्ति)

Written as code, not drawn.

A regeneratively cooled thrust chamber. Every channel, wall and manifold comes from rules the engine can check, so any variant is one parameter away.

02 / Parts · Yukti (युक्ति)

Every part, chosen and justified.

Agents pick the layout, the components and the alloy of each part, cite why, and log the decision.

03 / Physics · Vega (वेग) → Pramana (प्रमाण)

Cut it open. Read the physics.

Flow accelerates through the throat; the wall runs hottest there. Vega screens thousands of variants in seconds; Pramana’s self-checked CFD has the final word.

04 / Proof · Pramana (प्रमाण)

Then it has to survive.

Only designs that hold every margin at worst-case tolerance move on to print and test. Physics decides.

06Applications

One engine. Every machine that moves heat, fluid or force.

Domain packs teach the engine a new kind of problem. The loop, the rules and the ledger stay the same.

→Now

Liquid cold plates for AI chips

Direct-to-chip, single-phase plates for kilowatt-class GPUs: skived, machined and printed copper, judged against real chip limits.
